# Objects

Props keyed out of a reference and drawn as objects rather than as
photographs. `tools/key_handset.py` does the extraction, so anybody can
redo it and get the same file.

## `handset.png` — 132x525, YOUR CALL

Keyed out of the Hexa Creatives advertisement Ashley supplied: a black
handset hanging on its cord, on a light sheet, with their wording and
their address beside it. The script keeps only the blob the cord belongs
to, so none of their type came across, and nothing here reproduces their
layout, wording or credit.

**It is still their photograph.** This is the same exposure as the
Windows 95 icons in `assets/icons/pixel-flagged`, and it is a stronger
one, because a photograph is a single authored work rather than a common
visual idiom. Cutting the object out of their advertisement does not
make it Ashley's object, and web3ashley posts are commercial use.

It is wired into the `calling` template because Ashley asked for it,
having been told. If you want it off, delete this file: the block draws
the cord, the ring marks and the address without it and only the object
goes missing.

## The clean replacement, when there is time

`assets/stock/own/phone-chair.jpg` is Ashley's own photograph and it has
a dark green retro handset in it, held to his ear, and the matching
rotary base sitting on the chair arm. Both are his props on his sweep.
The base is the easier key of the two: it sits against the white sweep
with only the chair behind it, where the handset is against his hand and
his face. Swapping either one in changes nothing but this file.
