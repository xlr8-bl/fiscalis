# Topic backlog

One topic per line. `draft_article.py` takes the first unmarked line, drafts it
into `content/drafts/`, and marks it `[drafting]`. When you approve a draft and
move it to `content/articles/`, mark the line `[done]`.

Lines starting with `#` are ignored.

Write topics as the question a customer would actually type, or the problem in
their words — not as a keyword. "why does my website look different on my phone"
beats "responsive design best practices". The first is a real search; the second
is what everybody else already wrote.
